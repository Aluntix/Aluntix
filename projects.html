Yona from the valley

Yona from the valley is made in unreal engine, which utilizes 2D sprites in a 3D environment with the blueprints system which remembers settings the player has set perivously, along with a system that tracks the players progress to increase the difficulty while powering up the player as they progress.

The game is a 2D platformer with a focus on boss battles and story, with a variety of attacks and unique battle dialogue. The game is currently in development, and is expected to have a full release in the near future.
